# Dashboard (frontend/)

Mode: Operate. Users: founder (laptop), clinic owner (phone), clinic administrator (reception desktop). Jobs: founder onboards clinics, links WhatsApp, creates staff logins; admin works tomorrow's schedule and leads; owner reads the numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, familiar SaaS console (Linear/Stripe craft) that answers "who is coming tomorrow and what did Jazyl save" first; it refuses decorative dashboards with vanity tiles and chart walls.
OWN-WORLD: Milk ground #F7F5F0, white work surfaces with hairline warm-grey borders, a deep-teal #0F5E63 sidebar shell, amber #F2A541 only for the primary action (graphite text on it), Manrope headings, Inter UI text with tabular figures, status chips in teal/amber/red tints.
STORY: The admin sees tomorrow's list with live statuses and acts on reschedules; the owner sees saved tenge and confirmations; the founder sees every clinic's health and grants access.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Teal sidebar left (logo, clinic switcher for founder, nav). Main: page title + date, a row of four numbers (записей, подтвердили, переносы, сохранено ₸), then the schedule table for today/tomorrow with status chips; primary amber action top-right.
FORM: Canon (category standard) chosen by the user over the assigned departure-board direction; seed:6ec00d4ffe1f7dd0.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
